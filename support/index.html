Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to see the answer.

How do I add a camera to the app?

Tap the “+” button on the home screen, choose your camera type, scan the QR code on the camera or enter its ID, then select your Wi-Fi network. Full steps are on the How It Works page.

Is ICMob Camera free?

Yes, the app is free to download and use. Optional cloud storage plans may be offered separately.

Which phones are supported?

ICMob Camera works on Android 7.0 or later and iOS 13 or later, including tablets.

Why is my live video slow or blurry?

Check your Wi-Fi or mobile signal and try switching the video quality to Auto or SD. Moving the camera closer to the router also helps.

Why am I not receiving motion alerts?

Make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings, motion alerts are turned on for the camera, and battery saver is not blocking background activity.

Can I watch my camera from more than one phone?

Yes. Log in with the same account on other devices, or share access with family members from the camera settings.

Where are my recordings stored?

Recordings are saved on the camera memory card and, if you enable it, to encrypted cloud storage.

Are my videos private and secure?

Video streams are encrypted and your cameras are password protected. Only people you invite can view them, and you can remove access at any time.

How do I update my camera firmware?

Open the camera, go to Settings → Firmware Update and tap Update when a new version is available. Keep the camera powered during the update.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use “Forgot password” on the login screen and follow the steps sent to your registered email or phone number.

Step-by-step Guides

Short instructions for the most common tasks.

How do I share a camera with family?

Open the camera, go to Settings, choose the sharing option and invite a person by their account. They will see the camera on their own phone. You can remove their access at any time.

How do I turn on motion alerts?

Open Settings → Motion Alert and switch it on. Then choose a sensitivity level, and make sure notifications are allowed for the app in your phone settings.

How do I record to a memory card?

Insert a memory card into the camera, then open Settings → Storage to check it is detected and Settings → Recording to choose when the camera should record.

How do I change the Wi-Fi network?

Open Settings → Wi-Fi Settings, pick the new network and enter its password. Keep the camera close to your router while it reconnects.

How do I update the camera firmware?

Open Settings → Firmware Update. If a new version is available, tap Update and keep the camera powered until it finishes.

How do I view recordings from an earlier day?

Open Playback, pick the date on the calendar and tap a clip from the list.

Quick Troubleshooting

Try these steps before contacting us.

ProblemWhat to try
Camera shows “Offline”Check power and Wi-Fi, restart the camera and router, then pull down to refresh in the app.
Cannot add cameraUse a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, double-check the password and stay close to the camera while pairing.
No soundTurn up the volume, unmute the speaker icon and allow microphone permission for the app.
Playback is emptyConfirm a memory card is inserted and recording is enabled in Settings → Recording.
Night video is darkMake sure night vision is on and nothing is blocking the infrared lights.
