Live Monitoring in Real-Time

Watch what matters, right from your smartphone, anywhere in the world. ICMob Camera streams your camera feed directly to your phone with minimal delay, so you always know what is happening at home, in the office or at a shop.

  • Instant live view with one tap – no complicated setup
  • Smart connection switching between Wi-Fi, 4G and 5G
  • Full-screen landscape mode with pinch-to-zoom
  • Snapshot and quick-record buttons right on the live screen
  • Low-data mode for slow or limited mobile networks

Crystal Clear HD Video

Enjoy sharp, detailed and high-quality live video up to 1080P Full HD. Faces, number plates and small details stay clear, even when you zoom in on a busy scene.

  • Up to 1080P Full HD resolution on supported cameras
  • Smooth frame rate with adaptive bitrate streaming
  • Switch between HD, SD and Auto quality in one tap
  • Digital zoom with detail enhancement
  • Built-in night mode for clean low-light footage

Smart Motion Detection

Get instant alerts on your phone when any movement is detected. Choose which areas to watch and how sensitive detection should be, so you are notified about real events instead of every passing shadow.

  • Instant push notifications with a snapshot of the event
  • Adjustable sensitivity – low, medium or high
  • Custom detection zones to ignore busy areas
  • Schedule alerts for nights, weekends or travel
  • Automatic clip recording whenever motion is detected

How a Motion Alert Works

From movement to your phone in four simple steps.

Camera detects movement

The camera watches the zones you selected and reacts only when something moves there.

You get a notification

A push alert reaches your phone with a snapshot, so you can see what happened at a glance.

Check live or talk

Tap the alert to open the live view, or use two-way audio to speak to whoever is there.

Review it later

The event is saved in Playback with a time stamp, so you can find it on the calendar any time.
