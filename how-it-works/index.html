How It Works

Get Started in Just 3 Simple Steps

From download to live view in a few minutes – no technical skills or special tools needed.

  2. 2Add camera

See the steps

Before You Start

Keep these few things ready and setup will take only a few minutes.

Your camera

Plugged in and powered on. The camera ID or QR code is usually printed on its label.

Wi-Fi details

The name and password of the Wi-Fi network the camera will use.

A phone or tablet

An Android or iOS device with an internet connection to run the app.

Memory card (optional)

Lets the camera record locally, even when the internet is down.

Step 1

Install the App

Install ICMob Camera on your Android or iOS phone.

  1. Make sure your phone is connected to the internet
  2. Install and open the app
  3. Create your account with an email or phone number
  4. Allow notifications so you never miss an alert
Step 2

Add Your Camera

Connect your IP camera or Wi-Fi camera to the app. It takes only a couple of minutes.

  1. Power on your camera and wait for the ready signal
  2. Tap “+” in the app and choose your camera type
  3. Scan the QR code on the camera or enter its ID
  4. Select your Wi-Fi network and enter the password
  5. Give the camera a name, such as “Front Door”
Step 3

Start Monitoring

Watch live video anytime, anywhere – and let the app work for you in the background.

  1. Tap your camera to open the live view
  2. Turn on motion alerts and pick a sensitivity level
  3. Record clips and review them in Playback
  4. Share access with family or your team

Use It on Every Device

Once your camera is added, log in with the same account on any phone, tablet or computer and see the same cameras instantly. There is nothing to set up again.

  • Log in on a new device and your cameras appear automatically
  • Invite family or staff with their own access level
  • Remove a person’s access at any time

Your First Day with ICMob Camera

Once the camera is added, these four quick steps make it ready for daily use.

Name your camera

Use a clear name such as “Front Door” or “Shop Counter” so alerts are easy to understand.

Turn on motion alerts

Open Settings, switch on Motion Alert and choose how sensitive detection should be.

Try a recording

Record a short clip, then open Playback to see how saved videos are listed by date.

Invite your family

Share access with the people you trust so they can also view the camera from their phones.

Tips for the Best Experience

A few simple habits that keep your cameras working at their best.

Pick the right spot

Place the camera at eye level or higher, facing doors and main walkways. Avoid pointing directly at bright lights or windows.

Check your Wi-Fi

Keep the camera within good range of your router. A stable 2.4 GHz signal gives the smoothest live video.

Keep it updated

Install firmware and app updates when they appear. They add new features and improve security.

Add a memory card

A memory card lets the camera keep recording even if your internet goes down for a while.

Tune motion alerts

Use detection zones and medium sensitivity to cut down on false alarms from trees or traffic.

Save PTZ presets

On PTZ cameras, save positions for your door, gate and garden to jump between views in one tap.
