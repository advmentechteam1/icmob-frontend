Quick Answers

The questions people ask most. More on the Support page.

How long does setup take?

Most people add their first camera in under five minutes.

Can I watch my camera from more than one phone?

Yes. Log in with the same account on other devices, or share access with family members.

Where are my recordings stored?

On the camera memory card and, if you enable it, in cloud storage.

Will I get alerts when I am away from home?

Yes. Motion alerts arrive as push notifications on your phone over Wi-Fi or mobile data.
