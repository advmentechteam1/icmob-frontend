What Each Screen Does

The three main screens of the ICMob Camera app.

Live View

Watch your camera in real time with a clear Live badge and a full-screen button.

  • Snapshot, Record, Talk and Audio buttons under the video
  • PTZ pad to pan and tilt, plus Zoom controls

Home

See all your cameras and whether they are online at a glance.

  • Quick tiles: Live View, Playback, Devices, Alerts, Cloud, Settings
  • Bottom tabs for Home, Events and Profile

Recordings

Find any saved video by choosing a date.

  • Each clip shows its start time, length and file size
  • Filter the list and tap a clip to play it
